My name is Labhya Mehta, and I am a junior studying Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Cornell with experience in aerospace design and manufacturing. I am passionate about spaceflight mechanics, aeromodelling, and space systems. My interests lie in spaceflight mechanics, aeromodelling, and space systems, with a focus on integrating engineering design and computational simulation.

Through my internships at Boeing and Collins Aerospace, I have gained exposure to the aerospace industry and the engineering processes involved in developing projects.  I have applied tools such as CAD, ANSYS, XFLR5, MySQL, and Python to model, analyze, and visualize complex systems. My technical interests center on space systems engineering, flight mechanics, and the development of safe, efficient, and sustainable aerospace technologies.

On campus, I am actively involved in CUAIR, building unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as WOAA, ASME, and Astra Lab. These experiences have reinforced my ability to collaborate effectively, solve engineering problems creatively, and contribute to team-driven projects in dynamic technical environments.
