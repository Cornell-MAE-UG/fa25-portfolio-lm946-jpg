## About Me

My name is Labhya Mehta, and I am a sophomore studying Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Cornell with experience in aerospace design and manufacturing. I am passionate about spaceflight mechanics, aeromodelling, and space systems. My interests lie in spaceflight mechanics, aeromodelling, and space systems, with a focus on integrating engineering design and computational simulation. I have applied tools such as CAD, ANSYS, XFLR5, MySQL, and Python to model, analyze, and visualize complex systems. Through my projects and research interests in space systems engineering, I aim to contribute to advancing safe, efficient, and sustainable flight technologies.

Last summer, I interned at Boeing, where I designed and manufactured emergency doors for the Boeing 747. This summer, I will join Collins Aerospace as a mechanical design intern.

On campus, I am actively involved in CUAIR, building unmanned aerial vehicles, as well as WOAA, ASME, and Astra Lab. These experiences have reinforced my ability to collaborate effectively, solve engineering problems creatively, and contribute to team-driven projects in dynamic technical environments.
