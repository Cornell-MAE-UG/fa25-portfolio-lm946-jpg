## MAE 2250: Open Design Project 3

### Project Overview
This project involved the design of an automated sorting system. The system utilizes several mechanical and pneumatic components to move and sort material:

* **Roller with bristles**: Moves material into the collector bin.
* **Conveyor belts**: Facilitates the movement of items through the sorting stages.
* **Pneumatic Sorting**: Uses compressed air and vacuum zones to separate material into a trough.
